## Project Description

KRISHI-AI is an AI-powered agricultural intelligence platform built specifically for smallholder farmers in Tamil Nadu, India. The platform integrates 5 AI-powered tools into a single, easy-to-use web application that helps farmers make data-driven decisions about their crops, soil, markets, and weather.

The platform leverages Google Gemini's advanced AI capabilities — including vision analysis, multilingual text generation, and reasoning — to provide personalized farming assistance that was previously only available through expensive agronomists.

### Key Differentiators
1. **5 AI Tools in 1 Platform** — Unlike other farming apps that focus on just one feature, KRISHI-AI combines crop diagnosis, AI chat, market intelligence, soil analysis, and weather advisory
2. **Multilingual by Design** — Supports Tamil, Hindi, and English natively, making AI accessible to non-English speaking farmers
3. **Built for Indian Agriculture** — Contextualized for Tamil Nadu crops, markets, soil types, and weather patterns
4. **Professional Non-Generic UI** — Warm, earthy design that feels approachable and trustworthy, not cold and technical

## How Google Gemini is Used

### 1. Crop Doctor (Gemini Vision)
- Farmers upload photos of diseased crops
- Gemini Vision AI analyzes visual symptoms and patterns
- Returns disease identification, confidence score, and treatment recommendations
- Supports both organic and chemical treatment options

### 2. Krishi Guru (Gemini Pro)
- Multilingual AI chatbot for farming questions
- Supports Tamil (அ), Hindi (अ), and English
- Answers questions about crop selection, pest control, irrigation, fertilization
- Provides contextual, actionable advice powered by Gemini's reasoning

### 3. Market Intelligence (Gemini Insights)
- Analyzes historical price data and current market conditions
- AI-generated price predictions and demand forecasts
- Personalized selling recommendations
- Identifies best markets and timing for selling crops

### 4. Soil Analyzer (Gemini Recommendations)
- Takes soil parameters (pH, N, P, K, moisture) as input
- Gemini evaluates suitability for different crops
- Generates ranked crop recommendations with match percentages
- Provides fertilizer schedules (organic + chemical)

### 5. Weather Advisory (Gemini Advisory)
- Combines weather forecasts with farming knowledge
- AI-generated actionable farming advice based on weather
- Irrigation scheduling, pest warnings, harvest timing
- Seasonal farming calendar with AI recommendations

## Innovation & Creativity

### Original Ideas
1. **Unified Agricultural Platform** — First platform to combine 5 distinct AI-powered agricultural tools in one cohesive interface
2. **Mock Disease Analysis for Demo** — Hash-based image analysis that provides realistic, varied diagnosis results for demonstration
3. **Dynamic Weather UI** — Hero section and color scheme adapt based on current weather conditions
4. **Animated Circular Gauges** — SVG-based animated gauges for soil parameter visualization
5. **Language-Aware Chat** — Complete multilingual support including Tamil font (Baloo Thambi 2)

### Creative Design Choices
- Earthy agricultural color palette instead of generic tech-blue
- Farmer portraits and testimonials for authenticity
- Animated floating data pills in the hero section
- Kinetic title animations with word-by-word reveals
- Count-up statistics triggered on scroll

### Design Philosophy
- **Warm over Cold** — Earthy tones feel approachable
